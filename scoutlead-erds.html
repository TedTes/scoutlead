D2 · Audience, niche & market definition

Physical table territories = Audience in product terminology. niche_id is now the single authoritative business type. Exact clones are allowed; the former criteria uniqueness constraint and trade_keys were retired. service_keys and signal_keys are JSON selections resolved against the definition tables by application logic, not database FKs. Index segments describe shared supply by niche and market; they are no longer owned by a product.
grey stubs (other domains): businesses, products, seed_batches, source_observations, workspaces

D5 · Evidence & fact provenance

This is a branched provenance model, not a simple linear chain: source items receive decisions; observations attach collected evidence to businesses; claims and current facts derive from observations; fact changes preserve value history. Validation results independently record whether candidate identity, trade, location, and signal data passed their checks.
Red reliability boundary: ❶ source_observations — is the captured evidence accurate, complete, and fresh? → ❷ fact_claims — was the value extracted correctly with defensible confidence? → ❸ business_facts — does the reconciled current fact represent the business’s actual status? → ❹ validation_results — what did the provenance, replay, value, freshness, and conflict checks conclude? These are the reliability tables; source-item decisions and audience results are workflow consumers, not the reliability mechanism.
